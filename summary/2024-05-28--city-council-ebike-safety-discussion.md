## Summary
Council heard extensive public comment on e-bike safety, trail use, and broader multimodal transportation concerns.

## Key Points
- E-bike and multimodal transportation discussion
- Public comment on safety and trail use
- Debate over problem definition and possible responses

## Takeaways
Rather than adopting a single fix, council used the meeting to sort out what the actual e-bike safety problem is and which policy tools might fit.
