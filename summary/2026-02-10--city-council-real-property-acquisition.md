# 02/10/26 City Council Special Meeting

## Summary

Louisville City Council met solely to discuss a potential real-property acquisition. Council unanimously entered an executive session limited to appraisals, value estimates and negotiation strategy under the city charter and Colorado open-meetings law.

After returning to public session, council approved authorization for city staff to negotiate and sign an offer to purchase the property. Any resulting purchase contract would still require approval by council at a future public meeting.

## Key Points

- All seven council members were recorded as present.
- Council entered executive session to discuss strategy and valuation for a potential property acquisition.
- No final action was taken in the closed session.
- In open session, council authorized staff to negotiate and sign an offer to purchase.
- A final purchase contract remained subject to later public council approval.

## Takeaways

The meeting advanced a potential city property purchase while preserving a public approval step for any final contract. The property itself and negotiation details were not disclosed in the public portion of the meeting.
