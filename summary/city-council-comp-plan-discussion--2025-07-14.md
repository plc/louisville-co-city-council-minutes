## Summary
Special meeting focused on Louisville's 10-year comprehensive plan development. Council reviewed planning framework in segments to manage complexity.

## Key Points
- 10-year comprehensive planning cycle
- Segmented review process for thoroughness
- First of multi-meeting comp plan series

## Takeaways
The decision to "split up" comprehensive plan review across multiple meetings signals Louisville's commitment to thorough community planning rather than rushing adoption. The 10-year timeline represents significant long-term thinking amid rapid Front Range growth pressures.
