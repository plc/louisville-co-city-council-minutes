## Summary
Council held a joint special meeting with three advisory boards (Recreation, Open Space, and Parks & Public Landscaping) on April 8 to review board roles, align work plans with council priorities, and establish new engagement practices. Mayor Leiker emphasized the distinction between "advice and advocacy," clarifying boards should focus on sending recommendations to council rather than independent advocacy.

## Key Points
- Three sequential advisory board sessions (1 hour each): Recreation, Open Space, Parks & Public Landscaping
- Mayor clarified advisory role: boards provide recommendations, not advocacy
- New 2025 practice: boards encouraged to send representatives to council meetings
- Council will meet with boards multiple times per year (increased from annual)
- Staff updating bylaws for standardization across all boards
- 2025 council priorities: economic vitality, core services, safety, affordable housing (through equity/EDI and sustainability lenses)
- Recreation Advisory Board established 2017, newest of city boards
- City Manager Langley on day 7 of tenure

## Takeaways
The meeting represented a reset of council-board relationships under new leadership, with Mayor Leiker drawing a clearer line between advisory recommendations and independent advocacy—a subtle but significant shift in expectations. The new practice of board members attending council meetings aims to improve alignment and communication, addressing what may have been gaps in understanding council priorities.

The emphasis on standardizing bylaws across boards suggests prior inconsistencies may have caused confusion about roles and procedures. With City Manager Langley in her first week, the meeting also served as an orientation for new leadership on Louisville's civic infrastructure. The Recreation Advisory Board, as the newest board (2017), continues to find its footing on issues like golf course amenities (including the recurring "bathrooms on the front five" request Chair Norard mentioned) and broader recreation facility priorities.
