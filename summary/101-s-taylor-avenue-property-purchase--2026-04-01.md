## Summary

The City of Louisville published a walkthrough video of 101 South Taylor Avenue, a property it is considering purchasing for $13.25 million. The video is meant to help residents visualize the property before the City Council decides whether to proceed with the acquisition. Council is actively seeking community input via https://www.engagelouisvilleco.org/101taylor.

## Key Points

- **Property:** 7.96-acre site in the Colorado Tech Center; 56,054 sq ft building built in 2014 as Pearl Izumi's headquarters
- **Price:** $13.25 million ($236/sq ft) — sold as-is, furniture included
- **Cost comparison:** New construction in the region runs ~$1,000/sq ft, making this a significant discount
- **Building quality:** High sustainability standards, upcycled materials, energy-efficient systems; 85 parking spaces
- **Reason for purchase:** Current City Hall is at capacity and needs major repairs/upgrades; this property would address office space shortfalls and future community needs
- **Community input:** Council wants resident feedback before deciding — feedback portal at engagelouisvilleco.org/101taylor

## Takeaways

- This is a **major potential acquisition** — $13.25M for a new City Hall/municipal campus is the core ask
- The framing is favorable (cost savings vs. new construction), but the purchase still requires community buy-in
- Residents in Louisville should weigh in via the engagement portal if they have opinions on this
- No formal vote date mentioned in the description; decision is pending community feedback
