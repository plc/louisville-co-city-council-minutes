# Lewisville Police Department Recruitment Video

## Summary
The Lewisville Police Department released a recruitment video highlighting the benefits of working as an officer in the community. The 2-minute video emphasizes work-life balance, community support, modern equipment, and professional development opportunities that distinguish LPD from larger metropolitan agencies.

## Key Points
- **Department Size & Growth**: 35 officers strong and actively growing to serve 21,000 residents
- **Community Support**: Unusually high public support for law enforcement compared to other agencies
- **Work-Life Balance**: Emphasis on sustainable pacing with time for training, debriefing, and learning
- **Community Policing Focus**: Officers encouraged to develop professionally and build relationships with both colleagues and the community
- **Equipment & Resources**: 
  - State-of-the-art drone program
  - Ebike program
  - Taser 10 technology
  - Virtual reality training facilities
  - Extensive on-site workout facility
  - $1,600/year uniform allowance
- **Location Benefits**: 
  - 30 minutes from Denver (urban amenities)
  - 15 minutes from Boulder (mountain recreation)
  - Small town feel without urban crime pressures
- **Leadership**: Officers know their chief personally; flat organizational culture

## Takeaways
- LPD is actively recruiting and positioning itself as an attractive alternative to larger, more demanding police departments
- The department differentiates on quality-of-life factors, community relationships, and modern equipment rather than just compensation
- Strategic location between Denver and Boulder offers career benefits without the high-crime challenges of major metros
