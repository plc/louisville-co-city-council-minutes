# Louisville City Council - GDP Zoning Text Amendment
**Date:** June 3, 2025

## Ordinance 1896, Series 2025 - First Reading

### Purpose
>> Zoning proposal to amend Planned Community zone district regulations, allowing custom yard/bulk standards (height, setbacks, lot coverage) in General Development Plans (GDPs) without strict "general conformance" to underlying zone districts.

### Initiator
>> Community Development Director: "This is a proposal by PorterCare Adventist Health Systems. Um they initiated this ordinance amendment. It's a text amendment... they own two properties, zoned planned community zone district, but as a text amendment, it's broadly applicable to all properties in the city that have the same zoning."

### Current Problem
>> "The current regulations restrict GDP yard and bulk standards to be in general conformance with comparable zone districts... starting in 2010, there has been a lot of consistency saying that basically you have to match or be very similar to those underlying zone districts. So if you want to go outside of those standards, you really have to wait until you do the PUD and then you have to ask for waivers through the PUD process."

### Proposed Change
>> "These yard and bulk standards can be established on those general development plans um as long as they're consistent with the comprehensive plan or other adopted plan of the city and consistent with the desired character of the district."

### Rationale
>> "The purpose of that zone district and the purpose of creating general development plans is to use contemporary land planning principles and coordinated community design... we're creating custom zoning... that's contemporary land planning principles rather than just relying under the underlying zoning."

>> "The proposal would allow the city to adopt more comprehensive development standards within general development plans... It's still at your discretion as city council... the applicant's obligation to provide clarity on what they want to do and make the city council and the community comfortable with their proposal."

### Work Plan Alignment
>> "We do think it's consistent with this work plan item for city council which is near-term targeted priority development code updates to improve the city's development review process... this is an opportunity to provide a more businessfriendly approach to development review."

### Planning Commission
>> Reviewed May 8, "recommending approval as drafted."

### Council Questions
>> Council member Fahey asked about hospital zoning standards (no other hospitals in Louisville to compare). Staff indicated applicants would provide analysis comparing to other hospital developments.

## Notes
First reading. Public hearing to be set for second reading. This text amendment would apply broadly to all Planned Community zoned properties (30+ acres, single ownership, master planned developments).
