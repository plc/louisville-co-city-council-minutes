# Lewisville Police Department - Join Us!

With nearly 2,000 acres of open space as picturesque as this and ranked amongst the best places in the country to live and thrive, who wouldn't want to call the city of Lewisville, Colorado home? And when you work here, this is home. Welcome to the Lewisville Police Department. Every community has law enforcement services, but not every community supports those who serve and protect them the way the 21,000 people who live in Lewisville support us.

35 officers strong and growing. You'll be busy, but not too busy to enjoy a quality of life few agencies offer.

If you're looking for a department where you run call to call with mandatory overtime and no time for training or debriefing, look elsewhere. When pace of policing matters, look to Lewisville, where you're encouraged to learn and develop as an officer and have the opportunity to focus on community policing. You'll not just come to know your fellow officers. You'll know your chief and he'll know you.

But don't let our small family feel fool you. LPD boasts some of the best and most innovative equipment in the industry. From a state-of-the-art drone and ebike program to Taser 10 and virtual reality training, an extensive workout facility inside the building, and a $1,600 per year uniform allowance, you'll be outfitted with what you need here and more. Less than 30 minutes to Denver, but miles from the fast-paced, overcrowded, high crime that comes with a major metropolitan area. And 15 minutes to Boulder and a mountain escape. Lewisville is unique, but most unique is the way you'll be treated when you walk through these doors.

The Lewisville Police Department is proud to provide an environment where you'll do more than just meet the expectations of the job. You'll become exceptional.

Join us.
