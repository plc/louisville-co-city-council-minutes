## Youth Advisory Board History & Role

>> Board established: "the youth advisory board uh was first established by council in um 20 says approved by council on November 6, 2002 to advise city council on issues affecting the youth within Lewisville."

>> Original composition: "The first board consisted of one student from each grade from the following schools... they were appointed by school administrators. So, Cole Creek Elementary, Fireside Elementary, Lewisville Elementary, Lewisville Middle School, and Monarch High School."

>> Evolution: "open recruitment of youth advisory board members was started in 2005."

>> Current membership: "we have, I think, 16 members on the board this year... ranging from middle school to high school... we met for the first time in October of 2024 and we've been meeting monthly since then."

## All Careers Environmental Program

>> Description: "All Careers is a program in which it's about encouraging sustainability in not just the classic careers you would think are sustainable, like environmental careers, but careers all throughout, you know, the nation and the world. and just encouraging um practices within careers that encourage sustainability so we can limit the effects of climate change."

>> Led by: "It's run by an educator Martin Ogle. We've met with him multiple times on the board."

>> Field trips completed: "we did a nature walk and then one of them we got to go to um Jack's in Lafayette and then we also got to go to Alpin Windows and Alpin Windows was super cool because we got to see this type of window that is only made here in Lewisville."

## Notes
This was a special meeting focused entirely on the Youth Advisory Board's work. The board has 16 members ranging from middle school to high school, with some members serving up to six years. The meeting highlighted the board's autonomy in choosing projects while council encouraged more engagement on policy issues affecting youth, particularly comprehensive planning, downtown vision, and transportation safety (e-bikes, e-scooters).
