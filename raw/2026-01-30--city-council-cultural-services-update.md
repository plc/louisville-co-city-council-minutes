[CULTURAL SERVICES PRESENTATION - Brandy Cummings, Director]

**Department Overview:**
Cultural Services oversees three divisions:
1. **Louisville Public Library**
2. **Louisville Historical Museum**
3. **Arts & Events**

**Mission:** These "sister organizations" create sense of belonging and connection that roots people in Louisville beyond core city services.

**Stakeholders:**
- Three advisory boards (appointed by council)
- Two independent 501(c)(3) organizations:
  - Louisville Public Library Foundation
  - Louisville History Foundation

**LIBRARY HIGHLIGHTS**

**2024 Performance:**
- Service population: 32,000 (Louisville + Superior via IGA)
- Library card holders: 118% of population
- Expenditures per capita: $58/resident
- Circulation per capita: 58 items/resident
- Visits: 6.75 per resident (every resident visited ~7 times in 2024)

**Partnerships & Consortia:**
- Marmot (western slope + front range)
- Colorado Library Consortium
- Superior Intergovernmental Agreement (library services at Superior Community Center)
- Front Range Downloadable Library (Libby/Overdrive for ebooks/audiobooks)
- Economy of scale through shared purchasing and resource access

**2025 Successes:**
- Strong circulation and programming attendance
- Community engagement through events
- Resource sharing via partnerships maximizing tax dollar efficiency

**2026 Priorities:**
- Continue high-quality programming
- Expand digital resource access
- Strengthen community partnerships
