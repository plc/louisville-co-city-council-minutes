[ITEM 1: COOPERATION AGREEMENT]

**Background (City Attorney Kathleen Kelly):**
- Louisville Revitalization Commission (LRC) is an urban renewal authority created under Colorado law
- Fourth amended cooperation agreement updates the relationship between City Council and LRC
- Key changes from previous version:
  - Clarifies council's approval authority over urban renewal plans and TIF (Tax Increment Financing) agreements
  - Council retains final approval on bond issuance
  - LRC has more autonomy in project design/programming once plans are approved
  - Budget amendments under certain thresholds don't require council re-approval

**Discussion Points:**
- Delegation of authority: LRC can make programming decisions within approved budgets
- Council approval required for: new urban renewal areas, TIF agreements, bond issuance, major plan amendments
- LRC can proceed with detailed design and implementation once high-level plan is approved by council
- Agreement protects both entities' interests while streamlining project execution

**Outcome:** Agreement terms accepted; council comfortable with authority delegation framework

[ITEM 2: FRONT AND CENTER PROJECT - 30% DESIGN REVIEW]

**Project Overview (LRC + Staff):**
Downtown Louisville revitalization project focusing on Front Street (between library and arts center) and Main Street enhancements.

**Budget Status:**
- Original budget: ~$765,000 (TIF funding)
- Current 30% design estimate: tracking close to budget
- Concerns about cost overruns
- Council indicated willingness to provide contingency funding if needed (after cost-cutting attempts)

**Design Elements Discussed:**

**Front Street (primary focus):**
- Plaza/gathering space near library
- Enhanced landscaping and plantings
- Lighting improvements
- Potential splash pad (debated - not essential per some council members)
- Large tent infrastructure for events (questioned by Council Member Hamilton - expensive, concerns about utility)
- ADA accessibility features (benches, tiered seating vs. slopes)
- Food truck electrical infrastructure
- Bicycle parking

**Main Street:**
- Originally minimal improvements planned (sparked council concern)
- Council strongly suggested enhancing Main Street given its importance as "main avenue of Louisville"
- Options discussed:
  - Decorative pavers (expensive, likely cut)
  - Enhanced lighting and plantings (higher priority)
  - Balance between Front Street focus and Main Street visibility

**Key Council Feedback:**

**Council Member Hamilton (Mayor Pro Tem):**
- Disappointed at lack of Main Street investment initially
- Supports lighting and plantings on Main Street
- Not wed to splash pad if costs need cutting
- Concerned about large tent infrastructure and associated costs
- Prefer cutting elements before dipping into contingency funds

**Council Member Coopermanman:**
- Supports bicycle parking improvements
- Concerned about tent conflicts with farmers market
- Supports adequate electrical for food trucks
- Open to considering add-ons if brought back to council with specific requests

**Council Member Kern:**
- Supports budget discipline - prefer cuts before contingency use
- Questions expensive hardscaping value
- Concerned splash pad/library enhancements may not align with blight elimination mission (LRC's core purpose)
- Supports Main Street lighting and plantings
- ADA compliance essential (benches, accessible seating)

**Council Member Fehey:**
- Agrees with colleagues' points
- Supports flexibility

**Mayor Lei:**
- Supports all council members' feedback
- Comfortable with flexibility to economize and value-engineer
- Delegation of programming decisions to LRC accepted

**Direction Given to LRC:**

1. **Budget:** Council willing to consider additional contingency funding (~$765K range discussed)
2. **Process:** Must attempt cost cuts before requesting contingency funds
3. **Authority:** LRC delegated detailed design and programming decisions within approved framework
4. **New elements:** If LRC wants to add new features beyond current scope, bring back to council for approval
5. **Main Street:** Support for enhancing Main Street (lighting, plantings) - not leaving it untouched
6. **Cuts to consider:** Splash pad, large tent infrastructure, expensive pavers

**Business Community Input (Jillian - Chamber of Commerce):**
- Chamber submitted letter with feedback
- Appreciates LRC keeping business impacts in consideration
- Grateful for potential contingency support from city
- Farmers market coordination important
- Project looking fantastic overall
