# Louisville City Council Regular Meeting
March 18, 2025

## Public Comment: Inline Rink Controversy

Multiple residents testified against converting the inline skating rink to pickle ball courts.

>> Helmet Leinski: "I want to address the potential noise issue with the potential new pickle ball courts. I hope city council would reconsider getting rid of the inline skating park, which is a treasure that Louisville has. There's a lawsuit going on in Lone Tree against Parks and Rec because of noise. There's a moratorium in Centennial because of pickle ball noise. Denver has removed a few pickle ball courts because of noise."

>> Kyle Cheesebro: "I'm concerned about the planned inline rink conversion project which will eliminate numerous user groups in favor of pickle ball. At the time city council approved the CIP funding, the rec advisory board was pursuing two alternative locations for inline skating. City staff have recently acknowledged that each of those options will not work, which means skating and inline hockey users would be left with no viable alternative."

## Ordinances (First Readings - Public Hearing April 15)

- **Ordinance 1892**: Amending regulations for accessory dwelling units (ADUs)
- **Ordinance 1893**: Modifying definition of "family" in zoning code

## Resolutions 18 & 19: Landmark Designation - 509 Lefarge Ave

Lumpty House: 115-year-old coal miner housing with French immigrant history. Staff recommended $55K total in grants ($40K preservation/restoration + $15K new construction + $5K landmark bonus).

## Resolution 20: Wildfire Communication Campaign

Staff unanimously recommended Fors Marsh for $123K wildfire education campaign (repurposed mitigation coordinator grant funds). Campaign goals include WOOI code education, fire-wise practices, city mitigation efforts. Phased approach: develop materials → public outreach → hand off to staff.

## Parks/Open Space Long Range Plan (Pulled from Consent)

Staff proposed 17-month comprehensive planning process with ~30 stakeholder meetings. Timeline concerns due to monthly board schedules. Funnel approach: comprehensive plan → department plans → site-specific plans.
