# 02/10/26 City Council Special Meeting

## Opening and roll call

>> Good afternoon. This is a special meeting of the City Council of Louisville, Colorado. Why don't we take a roll call and then we'll move on here.

Council Members Dickinson, Fahey, Cooperman, Hefner and Kern, Mayor Pro Tem Hamlington, and Mayor Lake were recorded as present. Council recited the Pledge of Allegiance.

## Executive session request

>> We just have one item on our agenda, which is for an executive session for real property acquisitions and disposition.

The city manager requested an executive session under Section 5-2(c) of the Home Rule Charter and Colorado Revised Statutes Section 24-6-402(4)(a), specifically to discuss appraisals, other value estimates and strategy related to a potential real-property acquisition.

The city clerk read the required public statement:

>> It is a policy of the city to conduct public business in meetings open to the public. While in executive session, it is inappropriate to take straw votes, keep minutes, or make any final decisions.

>> If at any time the scope, nature or parameters of the executive session goes beyond the publicly stated topic, it is the responsibility of the council to terminate and cease any further deliberation within the executive session and return to the public meeting.

The statement summarized the subjects permitted in executive session, including legally confidential information, certain personnel matters, water rights and real-property acquisitions or dispositions limited to appraisals, value estimates and strategy, and legal consultation regarding pending litigation.

>> The request involves potential acquisition of real property.

A council member moved to enter executive session for real-property acquisitions and dispositions. The motion named City Manager Diana Langley, Deputy City Manager Sam Fox, Community Development Director Rob Zuccaro, Public Works Director Kurt Kowar, Finance Director Ryder Bailey, and City Attorney Kathleen Kelly as participants. The motion was seconded.

Council Members Kern, Hefner, Cooperman, Dickinson and Fahey, Mayor Pro Tem Hamlington, and Mayor Lake voted yes. The motion passed with more than five votes, and council recessed to executive session.

## Return to public session and action

After returning, the city attorney reported that the executive session had been limited to the announced real-property topic.

>> As follow-up to this topic, [I] recommend the motion that City Council make a motion to authorize city staff to negotiate and sign an offer to purchase property, subject to approval of the purchase contract by City Council at a public meeting.

A council member moved the recommended authorization and another seconded it. Council approved the motion without opposition.

Council then approved a motion to adjourn without opposition.
