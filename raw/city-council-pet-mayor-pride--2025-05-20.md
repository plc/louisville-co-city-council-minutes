# Louisville City Council Meeting - Pet Mayor & Pride Month
**Date:** May 20, 2025

## Pet Mayor Winner Announced
>> Mayor Leiker: "awful lot of public outreach, great uh public comment and uh visiting with the candidates. I understand it was difficult for some of the candidates. Um but uh Hazel came out on top. Great campaigner by the way. Uh she just has a way with people and uh understands the policies that are really important to our community for pets."

## Pride Month Proclamation
>> Mayor Pro Tem Dickinson: "I'm really really fortunate to get to read this. Uh I I am a father of three children. Uh, one is bisexual, one is lesbian, and one is non-binary. Literally, all three are queer. Um, and it's fantastic. They're wonderful human beings, and I love them all entirely, and to read this proclamation is is wonderful for me."

>> Proclamation states: "Louisville recognizes that visibility, advocacy, local engagement, and education are vital to creating a community where all individuals can live safely and free from discrimination... the city of Lewisville is committed to supporting the right of all its residents, regardless of their sexual orientation, gender identity, or gender expression to live... as their authentic selves."

>> Resolution: "we, the Louiswisville City Council, hereby proclaims the month of June 2025 as Pride Month in support of the LGBTQ plus community... the pride flag will fly throughout the month of June."

Proclamation approved unanimously.
