# Louisville City Council 2026 Work Plan Kickoff
**Date:** May 27, 2025

## Purpose & Process
>> Deputy City Manager Fox: "this is our first meeting to set the 2026 council work plan and priorities and will help us prepare for the supplemental budget conversation. We will also just a reminder that this council work plan reflects council work only. So it's beyond day-to-day services and it's focused on council policy issues for a year."

>> Duration clarification: "Do these have to be items that are completed within a year? Not necessarily. Many items on your current council work plan, for example, the comprehensive plan, last more than a year. They can still be something that's on your council work plan as long as you're actively working on them in that year."

>> Strategic framework: "Your 2026 work plan should be achievable, and it should maintain alignment with the resident survey, the comprehensive plan, the budget."

## Public Comment - Don Parker (378 Grass Court)
>> DEI & sustainability concerns: "I don't feel like it uh work plan as it's been envisioned addresses sustainability or DEI. Uh, I know we have the lenses, but I don't see DEI or sustainable sustainability showing up in the uh in the work plan that the the draft or whatever was out. So, I would ask for specifics, maybe add a couple columns for DEI and sustainability, specifically climate action and biodiversity."

>> Specific examples: "riding ebikes... Couple couple of young ones on the uh on the u one ebike and they're not looking. So, those are examples of specifics I'd like to see in the work plan that the council and staff would address."

## Public Comment - Katherine Smith
>> Multi-year projects: "there's a statement that's saying um the work plan will only include items that can be easily completed within a year and that triggered some things for me and I have three examples of very important things that probably can't be [completed in a year]."

(Full discussion paraphrased - meeting focused on setting priorities for 2026 work plan through council deliberation)

## Work Plan Framework
>> Strategic goals noted: "core services, safety, and housing" with "priority action items... specific um items that you've listed under the strategic goals."

>> Tonight's focus: "establishing the goals and aligning the goals with the resources... we don't expect this conversation to be finalized, and um we would go back summaries, submit it to council so that we can have additional followup."

## Notes
This was a special meeting dedicated to beginning the 2026 work plan development process. Full discussion and priorities to be finalized in subsequent meetings.
