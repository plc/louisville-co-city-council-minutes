[ITEM 1: BOULDER VALLEY SCHOOL DISTRICT PRESENTATION]

**Presenters:**
- Rob Price, Assistant Superintendent of Operational Services, BVSD
- Deanne Booker, Board of Education Member (District E - represents Louisville)

**Purpose:** Present community engagement plan for "Resilient Schools" initiative addressing declining enrollment and school consolidation planning.

**Key Context:**
- This is a presentation/information session - NO public comment tonight
- School district facing significant enrollment decline
- Planning process will require "systemwide adjustments" (potential school closures/consolidations)
- Focus on listening to community before developing any specific plan

**District Performance (Points of Pride):**
- BVSD performing at highest level ever
- Only metro Denver district accredited with distinction
- Graduation rates well above state average
- Student performance in English/math strongest in over a decade
- Declining enrollment NOT due to failing schools

**Enrollment Reality:**
- K-12 enrollment declining across Colorado for several years
- Largest single-year drop since pandemic occurred this past year
- BVSD not unique - statewide demographic trend

**Driving Factors:**
- Declining birth rates → fewer kindergarteners
- Aging population
- Low housing inventory
- High cost of living
- Families moving to more affordable surrounding communities

**Challenge:** Limited resources stretched thin across growing number of underenrolled schools, creating inequities in student opportunities and programming.

**Vision Alignment:** School district's "resilient schools" approach aligns with Louisville's comprehensive plan themes of resilience and sustainability (referenced by superintendent).

**BVSD Mission:** Create challenging, meaningful, and engaging learning opportunities so all children thrive and are prepared for successful, civically engaged lives.

**Enrollment Trends (2015-2025):** [Graph showed declining trend - specific numbers not captured in audio but visual presentation referenced]

**Louisville/Superior Region Focus:** Presentation looked specifically at interconnected Louisville-Superior school feeder patterns given proximity and shared attendance boundaries.

**Why Enrollment Matters:**
- Underenrolled schools = fewer resources per building
- Programming inequities between schools
- Staffing challenges
- Facility maintenance costs spread across fewer students
- Need to consolidate to maintain quality programming district-wide

**Community Engagement Plan:**
- Absolute focus on listening: teachers, principals, community, families
- Multi-phase engagement before any consolidation plan developed
- Transparency throughout process
- Recognition that these are "very difficult but honest conversations"

**District Response to Date:** [Presentation detailed past responses - specifics not fully captured but acknowledged earlier attempts to address declining enrollment]

**Council Questions/Discussion:** [Transcript shows questions occurred but specific exchanges not fully detailed in available audio]

**Alignment with Louisville Values:** Superintendent emphasized BVSD mission aligns with Louisville's comprehensive plan focus on opportunity, thriving community, and sustainability for future generations (who are currently in BVSD schools).
