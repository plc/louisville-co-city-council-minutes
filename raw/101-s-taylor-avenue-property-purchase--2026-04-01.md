The City of Louisville is considering the purchase of a 7.96-acre property at 101 South Taylor Avenue, seen in this video. Louisville City Council needs your help! Before deciding whether or not to complete this purchase, Council members want to understand the community's thoughts so that they can make an informed decision.

The property is being purchased as is, so all furniture seen in this video would be included in the purchase. More info: https://www.engagelouisvilleco.org/101taylor

ABOUT THE PROPERTY
Located in the Colorado Tech Center, the 7.96-acre property includes a 56,054 square foot building completed in 2014 as the headquarters of the Pearl Izumi company. The building was constructed to meet high levels of architecture and sustainability principles, using upcycled materials and energy-efficient systems. The property features 85 parking spaces, and the large lot size provides the opportunity for future expansion if needed.

WHY IS THE CITY EXPLORING THIS PURCHASE?
As the City of Louisville has grown, so has the City's needs for office space and additional community amenities. Louisville's current City Hall is at capacity and is in need of repairs and major upgrades. This $13.25 million purchase represents a unique opportunity to address multiple existing and future community needs at a cost of $236 per square foot — significantly lower than the cost of new construction. Other cities in the region have recently built new facilities at a cost of approximately $1,000 per square foot.
