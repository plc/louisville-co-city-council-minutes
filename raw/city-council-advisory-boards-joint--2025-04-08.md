# Louisville City Council Joint Advisory Boards Meeting
**Date:** April 8, 2025

## Meeting Format

>> Mayor Leiker explained: "city council joint meeting with uh uh a number of boards tonight. um the recreation advisory board first and the open space advisory uh board next and the parks and public landscaping advisory board known as peep lab will be third. We're going to do these um all in a row an hour each."

>> Purpose: "we've decided to meet with the boards and commissions um uh typically um uh hitting a couple of them on a Tuesday night and uh going over work plans plan items and kind of having a discussion about um uh things that are going well, things that can go better."

## Council Guidance on Advisory Role

>> Mayor Leiker clarified board function: "the boards and commissions. One thing we we are emphasizing and have been um is the importance of the boards and commissions advisory role. That's pretty much what the boards and commissions are doing... there is sort of a distinction at least that I draw and I think I think it's drawn in um our ordinances um between um advice and advocacy."

>> Expectations: "we're looking for recommendations and and and suggestions I think. Um uh but the boards and commissions are really hopefully going to send up recommendations to council to be u considered and acted on."

>> New practices for 2025: "we're going to encourage each board to send somebody to council meeting council meetings. Um uh we can talk about that at some point um to try to get a little better sense of what we're doing um as the year goes on. Um and uh we probably are are going to have maybe more than one meeting with uh boards and commissions per year um to kind of check in a little bit."

## Recreation Advisory Board Session

### Board Introductions
>> Lisa Norard (chair): "I've been on this board since its inception as well. So six or eight years ago. Um prior to that I was on the golf course advisory board. Um I'm passionate about all things recreation, golf, bathrooms on the front five. I have to just put that in there."

>> Dan Brener (new member): "I'm one of the newest members of the Wreck board. I started this January. Uh, I'm also fairly recent to Lewisville. I've been here for about a year."

>> Adam Blackmore (Director): "Adam Blackmore director parks recreation open space and golf doesn't fit in the acronym very well but uh don't want to forget it. I've been with the city almost three years now the entire time in this role."

>> City Manager Langley: "I'm Diana Langley, the city manager, and this is day seven on the job, and I can't think of a better way to spend my Tuesday evening."

### Board Role & Responsibilities

>> Municipal code duties: "the members of the board shall serve in advisory capacity to city council and shall have principal responsibility for matters related to recreation and golf programs and facilities."

>> Resolution responsibilities listed: "assist city staff and council in determining the community's needs and desires for recreation and golf facilities. Advise staff and council on maintenance, development, and redevelopment of recreation and golf facilities. Advise on capital and operating budget as it relates to recreation and golf. Um, again, advise on financial policies related to cost recovery for recreation program areas, on medium and long-range planning for recreation and golf facilities, and programming and facility usage for recreation and golf."

>> Governance reminder: "all city boards are subject to the Colorado Open Records Act and Open Meetings Rule and the Home Rule Charter."

>> Future standardization: "one of the projects that uh staff looks forward to working on over this next year is updating the bylaws so there's more standardization across all of the boards."

### 2025 Council Work Plan Alignment

>> Four priorities: "economic vitality, core services, safety, and affordable housing. All of those under um two lenses, including equity, diversity, and inclusion, and environmental sustainability."
